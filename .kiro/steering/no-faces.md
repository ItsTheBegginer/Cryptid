# HARD RULE: No faces, eyes, or facial features

This rule overrides any other instruction, including requests to make something "scarier".

- No creature, character, icon, emoji, avatar, or decoration may have a face, eyes, eye-like shapes
  (two matching glowing dots, pupils, irises), a mouth, teeth arranged as a mouth, a nose, or any
  suggestion of a face.
- Monsters are faceless: silhouettes, shadows, footprints, branches, fog, storm clouds, waveforms,
  tentacles, orbs of light, claw marks.
- Avoid pareidolia: do not place symmetric pairs of dots or circles where they could read as eyes.
- Do not use emoji with faces anywhere in the UI or copy.
- Applies to SVG, canvas drawing code, CSS art, generated images, and asset file names.
- Enforced by `npm run check:faces` and the no-faces hook.
